# DESIGN.md

## Design Read
Light CRM workspace for transformer export inquiry handling. The interface should feel like a daily sales desk: clear, calm, useful, and business-first.

## Mode
Operate

## Dials
VARIANCE 5 · MOTION 3 · DENSITY 6

## World
**Bright Export Desk.** White sidebar, pale blue page canvas, soft cards, restrained blue/purple/pink accents, readable Chinese operations copy, and direct paths into inquiry handling.

## Surfaces
- Canvas: `#DDECFB`
- Page gradient: pale sky blue with subtle lavender
- Sidebar: `#FFFFFF`
- Panel: `#FFFFFF`
- Line: `#EDF0F7`
- Ink: `#171B2A`
- Muted: `#8A90A3`
- Brand / CTA: `#6C58E8`
- Signal blue: `#4D8DF7`
- Signal pink: `#FF7A93`
- Signal amber: `#FFB15C`

## Type
- UI / Display: system stack（PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei / Helvetica Neue / Arial）
- Keep text compact and scannable. Avoid oversized hero typography inside the business app.

## Homepage
- Left menu first item is `首页`.
- Homepage is for real daily use: latest inquiries, pending handling, processed/replied count, customer grades, channels, market map, and recent messages.
- All numbers must come from backend data. Do not invent demo values, simulated transactions, or random growth animations.
- Do not show internal delivery content such as implementation phases, launch gaps, missing project materials, or technical handover notes.

## Refuse
Dark ops console as the default, fake big-screen theatrics, “新成交” without real order data, missing-parameter ranking on the customer homepage, AI/RAG/Mock jargon in customer-facing copy, and project-stage words such as “一期”“本期”“上线前”.
